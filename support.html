Common questions

How do I make a decision? Tap “Get Started,” list your options, add the attributes that matter, compare attributes two at a time to set their weights, score each option, and view your ranked results.

What is Decision Analyzer Pro? An optional auto-renewing subscription that unlocks unlimited saved decisions (the free plan keeps 3) and sharing/exporting your results. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings.

Do the AI suggestions require internet? No. AI runs entirely on your device using Apple Intelligence, and requires it to be enabled in Settings. The app is fully usable without it.

Where is my data stored? Everything stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
