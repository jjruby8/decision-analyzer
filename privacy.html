Decision Analyzer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Decision Analyzer is designed to protect your privacy. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

Data We Collect

None. Decision Analyzer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts and no analytics or tracking.

Your Decisions

Everything you enter — your options, attributes, comparisons, scores, and saved decisions — is stored only on your device. We operate no servers and cannot access your data.

Artificial Intelligence

AI suggestions (attributes and draft scores) use Apple's on-device Foundation Models (Apple Intelligence). Your inputs are processed entirely on your device and are not sent to us or any third party.

Subscriptions

Decision Analyzer Pro is an auto-renewing subscription purchased and managed through Apple. All payment processing is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
